SATF turns any file drag into a quick converter. Grab a file in Finder, give it a little shake, and a glass radial menu pops up right under your cursor. Drop the file on what you want and the new file is ready in a blink.

CONVERT IN ONE MOVE
• Images: HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, TIFF, BMP, AVIF, ICO
• PDF to JPG or PNG (one image per page), or to text
• Word, RTF, ODT, TXT, Markdown and HTML to PDF, DOCX, TXT and more
• Video: MOV to MP4, or pull the audio out as M4A
• Audio: M4A, WAV, AIFF
• ZIP files and folders, or extract ZIP archives
The menu puts the formats people pick most for that file first. Holding several files? ZIP comes first.

QUICK IMAGE EDITS
• Shrink a photo under a size you choose (1 MB by default)
• Compress, resize, rotate
• Remove location and camera info before you share
• Stack several images into one tall picture
• Merge images, PDFs and documents into one PDF, or split a PDF into pages

SHELF
Park files on the Shelf while you look for the next one, then drag them out together.

MADE FOR THE MAC
• Lives in the menu bar, stays out of your way
• Shake to open, or press a keyboard shortcut while dragging
• Glass design that feels at home on macOS
• Works offline. Your files never leave your Mac
• Originals are never changed. New files go to a folder you choose
• English and Vietnamese
