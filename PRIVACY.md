# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

SATF is a file converter that runs entirely on your computer. This policy covers the macOS and
Windows apps, including the version from the Microsoft Store.

## What SATF collects

**Nothing.** SATF has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, and no tracking. It does not
collect, store, or send any personal information.

## Your files

Files you convert, merge, zip, or extract are processed **locally** on your device. They are never
uploaded anywhere. New files are saved next to the originals, and the originals are never changed.

## When SATF uses the internet

SATF only connects to the internet when you choose to download an optional add-on:

| Add-on | Downloaded from |
| --- | --- |
| Office (LibreOffice) | `download.documentfoundation.org` (The Document Foundation) |
| Video & music (FFmpeg, Windows) | `www.gyan.dev` |

These are plain file downloads. SATF sends no information about you or your files. Those sites
may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

## Data stored on your device

SATF keeps its settings, downloaded add-ons, and an error log on your device only (for example
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\SATF` on Windows). The error log is never sent automatically. If you choose to
report a bug, SATF opens your own email app with a draft that you can read and edit before sending.

Uninstalling SATF removes this data.
